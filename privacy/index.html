Privacy Policy

Export ChatGPT Conversation · Last updated 7 August 2026

This extension exports a ChatGPT conversation to PDF. To do that, the conversation is sent to our rendering server and comes back as a file. We do not store your conversations, and we never sell any data. This page explains exactly what leaves your device, and what does not.

What the extension sends, and when

Nothing is sent until you press Export. When you do, two things happen:

  • Your conversation goes to our rendering server. The extension takes the conversation you are viewing — its text, and the images inside it, which it loads from your own ChatGPT session — and sends it over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to export-gpt.duckdns.org. That server turns it into a PDF and returns the file to your browser.
  • A usage event goes to Google Analytics. See “Usage statistics” below.

Conversations are processed only for as long as it takes to produce your PDF, and are not saved by us afterwards. Like any web server, ours keeps standard access logs — IP address, time, and request size — which are used to keep the service running and to prevent abuse. Those logs contain no conversation text.

Usage statistics

The extension sends three plain counters to Google Analytics so we can tell whether exports actually succeed for people:

  • export_completed — an export finished successfully.
  • export_selected_used — the “select blocks” export was used.
  • export_failed — an export failed, with a coarse reason such as “too large”, “network”, or an HTTP status code.

These events carry no conversation content, no page text, no titles and no web addresses. They are tagged with a random identifier that the extension generates the first time you use it and keeps on your own device (ga4_client_id in local extension storage), so that repeat use is not counted as a new person. It is not linked to your name, your email, or your ChatGPT account, and we cannot use it to identify you. Copies of the extension loaded locally for development send nothing at all.

What stays on your device

Your export settings — page format, margins, theme, scale and similar preferences — are saved in your own browser using Chrome’s storage API, together with the random analytics identifier above. This data is never sent to us. Removing the extension removes it.

What we never collect

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no passwords.
  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers or postal addresses.
  • No payment information — the extension is free and takes no payments.
  • No browsing history, and no reading of pages other than the conversation you choose to export.
  • No advertising, no advertising identifiers, and no sale or transfer of data to data brokers.

Why the extension asks for permissions

  • storage — remember your settings locally, as described above.
  • tabs — know which tab you pressed Export on, so the right conversation is captured.
  • access to chatgpt.com content and to oaiusercontent.com / images.openai.com — read the conversation you are exporting and load the images inside it, so they appear in the PDF.
  • access to export-gpt.duckdns.org — send the conversation for rendering and receive the PDF back.
  • access to google-analytics.com — send the three counters described above.

Who else sees this data

Only Google Analytics, and only the counters described above. We do not sell data, we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers, and we do not use it for anything unrelated to making this extension work. Our use of data complies with the Chrome Web Store Limited Use policy.

Our web pages

The welcome and uninstall pages on this site are ordinary web pages, separate from the extension. They use Google Analytics, which sets cookies in the normal way, to count visits.

Your choices

You can stop all analytics from the extension by removing it, or by installing Google’s Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. If you want the data associated with your random identifier deleted, email us and we will remove it.

Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
